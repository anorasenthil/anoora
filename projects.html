Smart City Coimbatore, Lakes & Race Course

Civic

Part of Coimbatore's first-round Smart Cities Mission, we delivered a ₹5–6 crore lighting scheme across six restored city lakes — Selvachinthamani, Valankulam, Kurichi, Kumaraswamy, Krishnampathi and Periyakulam — plus RGB and walkway lighting at Race Course. A deliberate 100% LED approach, using Philips, K-Lite and Keselec Schréder, keeps every waterfront consistently lit.

Thiruvalluvar Statue, Kurichi Lake

Civic

Built from 1,330 interlaced stainless-steel Tamil letters — one for every Kural written by Thiruvalluvar — this Smart City Mission landmark stands 20–25 ft tall on an island at Kurichi tank. We concealed K-Lite uplighters within the sculpture itself so every letter reads clearly after dark, without a trace of glare.

G.D. Naidu Bridge, Coimbatore

Civic

The elevated corridor runs along Avinashi Road, one of Coimbatore's primary urban arteries, connecting key commercial, residential, airport, educational, and industrial zones. A tribute to G.D. Naidu, renowned inventor and industrialist who significantly influenced Coimbatore's industrial development.

Horse Court Area, Coimbatore

Civic

Coimbatore's Kuthirai Vandi Court, a handsome British-era building that once kept vigil on horse cart owners, cranked to a halt in the 1990s. Resurrected from ruins, this building stands testimony to a time when horse carts served as last-mile connectivity.

Karthipuram Township, Neelambur

Commercial

A 250-acre private township of some 650 residential and commercial plots, schools and colleges, developed by Unnamalai Promoters. At the architect's request we matched the lighting standard of Bangalore International Airport — around 650 conical poles calculated with Keselec Schréder — and are completing a 15-metre media tower with imported Philips fixtures.

Darza Luxury Resort, Coimbatore

Hospitality

Set against the backdrop of the Sanjeevani mountain peaks, Darza Resorts combines sleek modern Scandinavian design with lush natural landscapes. Our lighting brief focused on highlighting the architecture's clean lines and the surrounding canopy of ancient trees, creating a sophisticated, tranquil evening atmosphere across the private villas, open-air dining spaces, and expansive lawns.
