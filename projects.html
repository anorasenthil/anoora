Civic

Public monument in Coimbatore dedicated to the revered Tamil poet and philosopher Thiruvalluvar, standing beside the scenic Kurichi Lake—a popular urban green space attracting visitors for leisure and cultural appreciation.

G.D. Naidu Bridge, Coimbatore

Civic

The elevated corridor runs along Avinashi Road, one of Coimbatore's primary urban arteries, connecting key commercial, residential, airport, educational, and industrial zones. A tribute to G.D. Naidu, renowned inventor and industrialist who significantly influenced Coimbatore's industrial development.

Horse Court Area, Coimbatore

Civic

Coimbatore's Kuthirai Vandi Court, a handsome British-era building that once kept vigil on horse cart owners, cranked to a halt in the 1990s. Resurrected from ruins, this building stands testimony to a time when horse carts served as last-mile connectivity.
