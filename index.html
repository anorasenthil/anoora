— 01
GOVERNMENT · SMART CITY MISSION

Smart City, Coimbatore

Coimbatore entered the Government of India's Smart Cities Mission in its first round, in January 2016, with lake eco-restoration, mobility and public infrastructure at its core. We executed a ₹5–6 crore lighting scheme across the city's ancient interconnected lakes — Selvachinthamani, Valankulam, Kurichi, Kumaraswamy, Krishnampathi and Periyakulam — now reborn as recreational waterfronts with walking tracks, boating and public plazas. At Race Course, one of the city's premium civic addresses, we specified RGB colour-changing systems and distinct walkway illumination for safety and aesthetics, choosing a 100% LED approach over solar for consistent, high-output performance.

— 02
HERITAGE & MONUMENT

Thiruvalluvar Statue, Kurichi Lake, Coimbatore

A flagship installation of the Coimbatore Smart City Mission, this 20–25 ft stainless-steel figure is built entirely from 1,330 interlaced Tamil letters — one for every Kural written by Thiruvalluvar. With natural gaps between each letter, the brief was to keep the form and script crystal clear at night without glare; we mounted K-Lite uplighters at calculated angles on all four sides, concealed within the sculpture itself, so the letters now read even more clearly after dark than in daylight.

PRIVATE TOWNSHIP · INFRASTRUCTURE

Karthipuram Township, Neelambur

A 250-acre private township in Neelambur, developed by Unnamalai Promoters with Ramani Sankar Architects — roughly 650 residential and commercial plots, alongside schools and colleges. The brief called for lighting on par with Bangalore International Airport, so we worked with Keselec Schréder on lux calculations and specified around 650 conical poles across the layout, an installation valued near ₹2.5 crore. We're also completing a 15-metre media tower with imported Philips fixtures from the Netherlands, plus the full indoor and outdoor lighting for the project's office.

LocationNeelambur, Coimbatore

05Approach

We believe light is a material — measured in lumens, but felt in moments.

Every brief begins on site. We walk the space, study how daylight enters, where shadow falls, how the room will be lived in. Specification follows feeling. The product list is the last document we write, never the first.

It is why we have remained a consultancy through three decades — not a catalogue.
