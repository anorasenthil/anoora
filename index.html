— 04
HERITAGE MONUMENT

Horse Court Area Coimbatore

Coimbatore's Kuthirai Vandi Court, a handsome British-era building that once kept vigil on horse cart owners, cranked to a halt in the 1990s. Over the years this masonry structure built with lime mortar and brick became a rundown dusty labyrinth, till it was taken up for renovation by the heritage division of the Public Works Department. Resurrected from ruins, this building on two acres of land next to Avinashi Road stands testimony to a time when horse carts served as last-mile connectivity for people from railway stations to towns.

TypeHeritage Monument
LocationAvinashi Road, Coimbatore

04Approach

We believe light is a material — measured in lumens, but felt in moments.

Every brief begins on site. We walk the space, study how daylight enters, where shadow falls, how the room will be lived in. Specification follows feeling. The product list is the last document we write, never the first.

It is why we have remained a consultancy through three decades — not a catalogue.
